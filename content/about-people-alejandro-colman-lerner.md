Alejandro Colman-Lerner is a geneticist and biophysicist, known for elucidating how cell-surface G-protein-coupled receptors can calculate the amount of ligand that binds them and transmit the fraction occupied to the decision-making machinery inside the cell (Bush et al., 2016). He is a tenured researcher at the Argentinian National Scientific and Technical Research Council (CONICET) and the University of Buenos Aires. Colman-Lerner is a close student of developments in AI, how those impact his scientific research program, how AI is developing in general, and how that development impacts the current security situation. He lives in Buenos Aires, Argentina.
