Michael Marletta is a biochemist. He is known for many things, but perhaps best known for his work on the biochemistry and enzymology of nitric oxide (NO). This work showed how NO was synthesized and mapped out the cellular machinery and chemical pathways through which it exerts its physiological effects. He has decades of experience advising the US government on matters related to security and follows developments in AI closely. He lives in Berkeley, California.
